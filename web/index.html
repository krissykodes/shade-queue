How the pit score works

For every empty street pit we "plant" a 10-year-old London plane (from a 2.5″ caliper, grown with USFS Urban Tree Database curves for Queens) and simulate its shadow on July 15, 2–4 pm, every 30 minutes.

Only shadow that lands on sidewalk that is sunny today counts as new shade: not under a building's shadow and not under an existing street tree's shadow (LiDAR-measured crowns).

New shade (m²·h) = average new sidewalk shade × 2 hours. Pits are ranked by it. Yield = the share of the tree's shadow that is new shade.

Heat vulnerability is shown as published (DOHMH, by neighbourhood) and is not mixed into the score: Parks already prioritises neighbourhoods by HVI; this ranks pits within them.
